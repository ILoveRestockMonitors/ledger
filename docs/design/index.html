Ledger · homepage mockups

Three design directions

Non-functional homepage mockups built from a study of 18 finance products and 14 animation-led sites, plus a phone adaptation of the futuristic direction. Each is a self-contained HTML file on the same curated dataset, so they compare like-for-like. Every bar, bubble, meter and row responds to a click. The study notes are in RESEARCH.md.

AuroraFuturistic

Cinematic dark glass. A fixed aurora field with grain, translucent surfaces over it, a 76px net-worth figure, and a spend constellation whose bubbles drift until you press one.

Aurora · phoneMobile

The same tokens and motion contract at 390 × 844. Bottom-sheet detail instead of an inline accordion, a snap-scrolling account rail, 64px rows, and the app's real four-tab navigation. Shown in a device frame on desktop; full-bleed on an actual phone.

QuietMinimalist

Hairlines and one indigo, nothing else. No gradients, no shadows, radius capped at 8px. Density where the data lives, generosity around the chrome — spend bubbles reduced to circles resting on a single axis.

AlmanacThe surprise

The month set as a printed broadsheet — letterpress rules, engraved hatch columns, ink-blot spend discs, and a risograph overprint that sits out of register until you press something and it snaps into place. Built on Ledger's own paper, Lora and copper.

Implementing these? docs/design/handoff/ holds the build brief and screenshots.
Every mockup carries both a light and a dark mode. Each follows your system preference on load and can be switched with the control in its own header; the choice is held in memory only. Mockups otherwise are just mockups — no network, no storage, no backend. All figures are illustrative. Motion follows one contract across all three: ease-out on entrances, nothing over 300ms, transform and opacity only, and everything disabled under prefers-reduced-motion.
